# Pocket Guild Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026

Pocket Guild is published by **Hussein Elbeheiry**. For privacy questions, contact **[hussein@elbeheiry.dev](mailto:hussein@elbeheiry.dev)**.

## Information stored on your device

Pocket Guild stores game progress and preferences locally on your device. This includes heroes, equipment, gold, world progress, tutorial status, and sound and haptic preferences. The app does not operate an account service or send gameplay, personal information, or device identifiers to the developer. It has no advertising, tracking, or analytics SDKs.

Local data remains until you remove the app. iOS backups may include this data according to your Apple backup settings. Apple manages those backups under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Save export and recovery

You may export save files using the iOS share sheet. You choose the recipient or service, whose privacy terms apply to that transfer. Restoring a save archives the previous files locally to protect your progress. Removing the app removes its local saves and recovery archives; existing device or cloud backups are managed through Apple’s settings.

## Support and diagnostics

If you contact support, the developer receives the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request and investigate the reported issue. Do not include sensitive personal information in a support attachment. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. Contact the email above to request deletion of correspondence you have sent.

Apple may provide crash or diagnostic reports according to your device or TestFlight sharing settings. The app does not include a third-party crash-reporting SDK. Opening our GitHub policy or support pages is subject to GitHub’s own privacy policy.

## Game timing

The app uses elapsed system time for foreground game timing. Combat and income pause in the background. The app does not request location, contacts, camera, or microphone access.

## Children

The app does not create user accounts or knowingly collect children’s personal information through gameplay. A parent or guardian may contact support regarding information voluntarily sent to the developer.

## Updates and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. Questions and privacy requests: [hussein@elbeheiry.dev](mailto:hussein@elbeheiry.dev).
