# Pocket Guild Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 10, 2026

Pocket Guild is published by Hussein Elbeheiry. For privacy questions, contact hussein@elbeheiry.dev.

## Information stored on your device

Pocket Guild stores game progress and preferences locally on your device. This includes heroes, equipment, gold, world progress, tutorial status, and preferences. No game account is required. Game progress is not uploaded to the developer. The app has no advertising SDKs and does not use purchase data for cross-app advertising tracking. Purchase management uses RevenueCat as described below.

Local data remains until you remove the app. iOS backups may include this data according to your Apple backup settings. Apple manages those backups under its own terms and privacy policy.

## Save export and recovery

If you choose to copy or share a save file, you choose the recipient or service, whose privacy terms apply to that transfer. The app keeps a local backup of recent saves for recovery. Removing the app removes its local data; existing device or cloud backups are managed through Apple's settings.

## Purchases and RevenueCat

Apple processes the optional one-time Full Game Unlock. We use RevenueCat to validate purchases, restore access, handle purchase-status changes such as refunds, and report sales. RevenueCat receives purchase and transaction information, a randomly generated app customer identifier, and technical app, operating-system, and store information needed to provide the service. This is used for app functionality, fraud prevention, and purchase analytics. The app does not send RevenueCat your name, email address, or gameplay save, and disables the SDK's automatic device-identifier collection.

RevenueCat may communicate with its servers when the app opens and during purchase or restore operations, including before a purchase is made. Purchase ownership information is also cached on the device. Apple handles payment details; the developer does not receive your payment-card information. Restoring a purchase recovers ownership, not your locally stored game progress.

RevenueCat processes purchase information under its privacy policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy. Apple processes purchases and backups under its own policies: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/. Contact the developer below with questions or requests concerning purchase information associated with this app. Purchase records may need to be retained for restoration, refunds, and applicable store obligations.

## Support and diagnostics

If you contact support, the developer receives the email address, message, and attachments you choose to send. This information is used to answer your request and investigate the reported issue. Do not include sensitive personal information in a support attachment. Support correspondence is retained as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable obligations. Contact the email above to request deletion of correspondence you have sent.

Apple may provide crash or diagnostic reports according to your device or TestFlight sharing settings. The app does not include a third-party crash-reporting SDK. Opening our GitHub policy or support pages is subject to GitHub’s own privacy policy.

## Game timing

The app uses elapsed system time for foreground game timing. Combat and income pause in the background. The app does not request location, contacts, camera, or microphone access.

## Children

The app does not create game accounts or knowingly request identifying information from children during gameplay. A parent or guardian may contact support regarding information voluntarily sent to the developer.

## Updates and contact

This policy may be updated as the app changes. The effective date above identifies the current version. Questions and privacy requests: hussein@elbeheiry.dev.
