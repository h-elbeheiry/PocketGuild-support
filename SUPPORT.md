# Pocket Guild Support

Published by **Hussein Elbeheiry**.

For help, bug reports, accessibility issues, or privacy requests, email **[hussein@elbeheiry.dev](mailto:hussein@elbeheiry.dev)**.

Include your device model, iOS version, Pocket Guild version, and the steps that led to the problem. Screenshots are helpful; please remove personal information before sharing them.

## Save problems

Open **Settings → Your adventure**. If saving failed, try **Retry saving or recovery** after freeing device storage. You can export save files or restore a valid file. Restoring archives the previous files on the device and leaves the game paused until you resume.

Do not uninstall the app while troubleshooting if you need its local progress. Email support before sharing a save; send only the information needed to investigate the issue.

## Common questions

- **Does it need an internet connection?** No. Gameplay and saves work locally.
- **Does combat continue when I close the app?** No. Combat and income pause in the background.
- **Does progress sync between devices?** Automatic cross-device sync is not currently supported. Settings supports save export and restore.
- **Does it have ads or in-app purchases?** No.
